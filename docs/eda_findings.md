## 1. Fraud is rare -- accuracy is a useless metric here

- Overall row-level fraud rate: **0.2595%** across **5,049,290** rows.
- A model that predicts 'never fraud' would be ~99.7% accurate and catch zero fraud. This is why the project scores with AUC and, later, dollar-weighted cost -- never raw accuracy.
